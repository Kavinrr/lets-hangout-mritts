What's the Vibe?

Regardless of which adventure — this is what the day actually feels like.

  • Sitting quietly in a park without feeling the need to constantly talk.
  • Nudging each other's shoes while sitting on a bench.
  • Taking candid photos together.
  • Catching up on everything we've missed.
  • Walking without any destination in mind.
  • Getting dessert after we've already said we're full.
  • Rating random dogs we meet on the street.
  • Going into random bookstores or record stores "just for five minutes" and inevitably staying longer.
  • Making spontaneous detours if we spot something interesting.
  • Simply enjoying each other's company without worrying about having a perfect plan.

The Point

No itinerary. No pressure. Just time together that doesn't need to be anything more than that.
